# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and professional community for everyone participating in this project. Contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to communicate constructively, respect differing perspectives, and focus discussions on improving the website and its visitor experience.

## Expected behavior

Participate in good faith, provide actionable feedback, respect privacy and intellectual property, and help keep discussions accessible and inclusive. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberately disruptive conduct, and the sharing of private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through [TechTide AI](https://techtideai.io). Maintainers will review credible reports promptly and may take proportionate action, including requesting a correction, removing content, limiting participation, or excluding a participant from project spaces. Retaliation against a reporter is not acceptable.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project-related interactions.
